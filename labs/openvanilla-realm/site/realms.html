Verified P2P nodes

Nodes are volunteers' relays: players connect through them, so hosts open no ports and nobody's address is shown. They carry only end-to-end encrypted game traffic. Supporters & how to become one
Play without Leyline. If this list is down, Leyline is offline: you can still host and join friends directly. In the launcher, host with “Direct from my home connection” or “My local network only” and send a guest invite; friends paste it in the Realms tab or use Join by address. In direct mode your friends see your home IP address, so only invite people you trust.

Private realms aren't listed; they're joined with an invite link from their host. Ping isn't shown here: the launcher measures it from your PC.
