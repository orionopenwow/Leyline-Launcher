## Play now {#play-now}

Open **[play.openvanilla.app/wrath](https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath)** and press **Play**. That is the **Dalaran Hangout**, Leyline's sample realm: a meeting place to try Leyline, meet other players and see the newest features as they land. Nothing to install; your browser streams the game.

Sign in once and the page remembers you. The [Leyline hub](https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline) lists everything else: other realms, mods and how to host your own.

## What you need {#requirements}

A current desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and a steady broadband connection. The game runs on Leyline's server and streams to your tab, so your computer does not need to be fast.

## Phones and tablets {#touch}

Open the same address on a phone or tablet. A keyboard and mouse work best.

## Other realms {#realms}

The realm list at **[play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline/realms](https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline/realms)** shows every realm on the Leyline network, including ones players host themselves. Want your own? See [Host your own realm](hosting.html).

## If something goes wrong {#trouble}

- The page stays on "loading": refresh once, then try another browser.
- You can't sign in: clear the site's cookies for play.openvanilla.app and sign in again.
- Still stuck: ask in the Leyline Discord (see [Community](community.html#tickets)).

> **Note:** The browser realm that used to run from this site is retired. Its page now takes you to the Dalaran Hangout.
