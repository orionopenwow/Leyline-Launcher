## Common questions {#faq}

### Is this official? {#official}
No. Leyline is a free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard. World of Warcraft is a trademark of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.

### Does it cost anything? {#cost}
No. Leyline is free, and nothing in it is for sale.

### Why do I need my own 1.12.1 game client? {#own-client}
Leyline never distributes the game's files and never downloads a client. Use the 1.12.1 client you already have, or play in your browser.

### Will I lose my characters? {#characters}
No: characters live on the realm. Guests keep theirs by linking Discord. Solo characters live on your PC. Alpha realms may be reset; that's announced first.

### Can I play on my phone? {#phone}
Yes, in the browser ([Phones and tablets](browser.html#touch)).

### Can I use my old addons? {#old-addons}
Many Vanilla addons work. Install them from the Workshop or a zip; the Workshop label tells you how well they're expected to work.

### Does Leyline use AI? {#uses-ai}
It has no AI of its own. You can connect your own AI agent to build things ([Connect an AI](ai.html)); Herald can optionally help staff sort reports, and humans decide every action.

### How do I delete my data? {#delete-data}
Ask on Discord. See [privacy](../privacy.html).
