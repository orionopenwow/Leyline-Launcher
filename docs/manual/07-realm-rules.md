summary: The Vanilla Remastered realm is retired. Play on the Dalaran Hangout instead.

The **Vanilla Remastered** realm this chapter described is retired, along with its rules, Star Pouches and realm achievements.

Play now on the **Dalaran Hangout**, Leyline's sample realm: open **[play.openvanilla.app/wrath](https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath)**. Other realms, including ones players host themselves, are on [the realm list](https://play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline-preview/realms). To run your own, see [Host your own realm](hosting.html).
