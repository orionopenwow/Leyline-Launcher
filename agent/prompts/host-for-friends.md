# Host a realm for friends

Step-by-step help getting Leyline Server running and your friends connected.

Works on: Your PC and your own realm.

## Prompt

```text
Read CLAUDE.md. Help me run my own realm for friends with Leyline Server (openvanilla.app/host.html). Check whether SPP Classics is set up (Realms\Solo or C:\SPP_Classics_V2), walk me through Leyline Server one step at a time, explain the choice between my home network and the internet (the internet shows my IP address to the people I invite), and if something fails, read Leyline Server's logs and tell me what to fix. Don't change my router or firewall yourself: tell me what to do.
```

## Tips

- Only give your address to people you trust, and halt the realm when you are done.
