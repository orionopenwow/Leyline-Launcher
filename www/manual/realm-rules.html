    Chapter 7 · Play

    The Vanilla Remastered realm

    The Vanilla Remastered realm is retired. Play on the Dalaran Hangout instead.

    The Vanilla Remastered realm this chapter described is retired, along with its rules, Star Pouches and realm achievements.

    Play now on the Dalaran Hangout, Leyline's sample realm: open play.openvanilla.app/wrath. Other realms, including ones players host themselves, are on the realm list. To run your own, see Host your own realm.
