    Playing, hosting and the realm list live on the Leyline hub:

    • Play now: the Dalaran Hangout, Leyline's sample realm, at play.openvanilla.app/wrath.
    • Host your own: play.openvanilla.app/wrath/host (Host your own realm).
    • Realm list: play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline-preview/realms.
    • Everything else: the Leyline hub.

    Retired pages #

    The pages that belonged to the earlier realm are retired: they now send you to the Leyline hub. That covers the play page and the host guide, the Armory, Guilds, the old realm list and node pages, Streamers and Creators, Herald, Boss Fights, Duels, Legend and Moderation.

    The Armory #

    The Armory showed characters from the earlier realm. It is retired with that realm.

    Who could see your character #

    Nothing new is published. Character pages, rankings and guild pages are no longer served.

    Guilds #

    The guild pages were part of the Armory and are retired with it.

    Streamers and creators #

    The streamers and creators pages listed channels for the earlier realm. They are retired.

    Link your channel #

    Channel linking ended with the earlier realm. Nothing more is collected.

    The LIVE mark in game #

    The in-game LIVE mark needed the earlier realm's server and no longer shows.

    Share what you're listening to #

    Off by default. In the classic launcher, Settings → Privacy turns it on or off. When on, your Persona shows "Listening: title – artist", nothing more.

    Your privacy #

    The short version of privacy.html:

    • No ads, no analytics, nothing sold.
    • The realm stores a password verifier, never your password. The launcher can remember your password encrypted with Windows' own protection, and sends no crash reports or files.
    • Sign in with Discord only gets your identity and permission to add you to the server; it never sees your Discord password or messages.
    • Herald keeps your Discord ID and name, your join and gate dates and your adventurer number.
    • You must be 13 or older. To delete your data, ask on Discord.

    The terms in one line: it's a free, non-commercial fan project with no Blizzard files; no harassment, cheating, exploits or attacks; one person per account; accounts may be reset during the alpha; Workshop content belongs to its authors.
