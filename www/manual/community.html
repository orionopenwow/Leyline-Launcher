    Community, Discord and Herald

    The Discord server and its gate, Herald the bot, world chat between Discord and the game, tickets, reports.

    The Discord server #

    The Leyline Discord is where the community talks, gets help and hears news first. It's also how you get a realm account: Discord sign-in creates it (see Getting started).

    #looking-for-group and #trade have a one-minute slow mode; #guild-recruitment allows one post per guild every six hours.

    Roles #

    • Access: Outsider → Recruit → Adventurer (see the gate, next).
    • Badges: Level 60, Trailblazer (the first 500 Adventurers, kept forever), Supporter (tipped the realm; never buys anything in game), Creator, Alpha Tester, Beta Tester, Guild Master.
    • Faction and class: Alliance or Horde, plus your main class. Pick them with the buttons and menu in #welcome; your name takes your class colour.
    • Staff: Founder, Admin, Developer, Game Master, Moderator. Staff will never ask for your password.

    The gate: Outsider, Recruit, Adventurer #

    New members start as Outsiders and see only #welcome. This keeps bots and raids out.

    1. Enter the gate. Press the button in #welcome and answer a random question about the rules (four choices). A wrong answer means a 30-second wait; three misses lock the gate for 15 minutes. Outsiders who never pass are removed after 48 hours.
    2. Recruit. You can now read Start Here, ask in help, pick your faction and class, and press Create my account.
    3. Adventurer. Creating your realm account with Sign in with Discord makes you an Adventurer: full access, and Herald announces you in #arrivals (silently: no pings, ever) with your adventurer number, class and faction.

    Note: If many people join at once (8 within a minute), Herald raises a raid shield: the gate closes and invites pause for about 15 quiet minutes. Just try again later.

    Herald #

    Herald was the earlier realm's bot: the Discord gate, the server-status message, the chat bridge and the nightly test runs. He is retired with that realm, and his code is kept in the Leyline-Launcher repository (labs/herald).

    Herald's Discord commands #

    Herald's commands are retired with him.

    Let Herald play #

    Herald Autopilot is a desktop app for the classic launcher's own and test realms (Herald Autopilot.bat). It isn't used on the Leyline hub or the Dalaran Hangout.

    World chat between Discord and the game #

    The bridge between Discord and the earlier realm's World chat is retired.

    Link your guild's chat #

    Guild chat links were part of that bridge and are retired too.

    Get help: tickets #

    Stuck, or something doesn't work? Post in the help forum on the Leyline Discord: what you did, what you expected and what happened. On a realm someone hosts, its host or staff can help with characters and accounts.

    Report a bug #

    Use the bug-reports forum on Discord: what happened, what you expected, the steps to make it happen again, and a screenshot if you can. Staff can turn a good report straight into a tracked issue. Security problems go privately instead: see security.html.

    Report a player #

    Report players to the staff of the realm you play on. On the Dalaran Hangout and the Leyline Discord, use the Discord's report option or the help forum. Humans decide every case.
