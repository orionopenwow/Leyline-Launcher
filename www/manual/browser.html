    Chapter 2 · Play

    Playing in the browser

    Play now in a browser tab: the Dalaran Hangout, Leyline's sample realm, with nothing to install.

    On this pagePlay nowWhat you needPhones and tabletsOther realmsIf something goes wrong

    Play now #

    Open play.openvanilla.app/wrath and press Play. That is the Dalaran Hangout, Leyline's sample realm: a meeting place to try Leyline, meet other players and see the newest features as they land. Nothing to install; your browser streams the game.

    Sign in once and the page remembers you. The Leyline hub lists everything else: other realms, mods and how to host your own.

    What you need #

    A current desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and a steady broadband connection. The game runs on Leyline's server and streams to your tab, so your computer does not need to be fast.

    Phones and tablets #

    Open the same address on a phone or tablet. A keyboard and mouse work best.

    Other realms #

    The realm list at play.openvanilla.app/wrath/leyline-preview/realms shows every realm on the Leyline network, including ones players host themselves. Want your own? See Host your own realm.

    If something goes wrong #

    • The page stays on "loading": refresh once, then try another browser.
    • You can't sign in: clear the site's cookies for play.openvanilla.app and sign in again.
    • Still stuck: ask in the Leyline Discord (see Community).

    Note: The browser realm that used to run from this site is retired. Its page now takes you to the Dalaran Hangout.
