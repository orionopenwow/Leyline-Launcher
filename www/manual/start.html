    Getting started

    What Leyline is, the ways to play, what you need, and your account.

    What Leyline is #

    Leyline is a free, open-source mod launcher and peer-to-peer realm network. Anyone can host a realm in one click, from the browser or the desktop; players find it on the shared realm list and join, and mods come along. Try it now on the Dalaran Hangout, Leyline's sample realm.

    Note: Leyline is a fan project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard, it sells nothing, and it never ships Blizzard's game files.

    This manual also covers the classic Leyline Launcher, the earlier desktop app, which existing players still use.

    Quick start: browser #

    1. Open play.openvanilla.app/wrath.
    2. Press Play and sign in.
    3. You're on the Dalaran Hangout.

    Details: Playing in the browser. Want your own realm? Host your own realm.

    Quick start: launcher #

    1. Download the launcher from the home page (Desktop Client): OpenVanilla-Setup-<version>.exe is the recommended installer, or the zip for a portable copy.
    2. Start it. On Home, the bar above PLAY says what's missing; pressing PLAY fixes it: it lets you pick the folder of the 1.12.1 client you already have, or play in your browser.
    3. Press PLAY. The first time, it signs you in with Discord.

    Details: The launcher.

    Note: The launcher isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say "Unknown publisher" or show SmartScreen. Choose More info → Run anyway, or check the file against the checksums on the release page first.

    Your account #

    • On the Leyline hub and the Dalaran Hangout you sign in on play.openvanilla.app.
    • A realm someone hosts has its own accounts: its host tells you how to join.
    • Staff will never ask for your password.

    What you need: Vanilla Client v1.3 #

    The desktop game needs your own copy of Vanilla Client v1.3 (English, build 5875). The launcher's game setup page checks the folder of the client you already have (Choose its folder), or switches you to browser play. It never downloads a client. It never changes the game's files. More on clients.html and in The launcher.
