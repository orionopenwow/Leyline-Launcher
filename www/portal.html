Leyline

A mod launcher and a peer-to-peer realm network. Host your own realm in one click, from the browser or the desktop.

Players find it on the shared realm list and join, and the mods come with it. Free and open source.

How Leyline works

Every realm is someone's own. Leyline connects them: it carries players to the host, so nobody opens ports and nobody sees anybody's address.
